## **Project Description**
This is a complete **School Management System** built in **Python**.  
It provides a backend system to manage students, teachers, attendance, fees, and salaries.  
The project is designed with **OOP principles**, **data persistence**, **logging**, and **error handling**.  

It is production-ready and can be used as a foundation for building a **frontend GUI** with **Tkinter** or a **web interface** in the future.  

---

## **Features**

### 🎓 Student Management
- Add, update, remove, and search students  
- Track attendance and calculate attendance percentages  
- Manage fees: pay, track due, generate receipts  
- Guardian details (optional)  

### 👩‍🏫 Teacher Management
- Add, update, and manage teacher profiles  
- Mark attendance  
- Process salary with bonuses and deductions  
- Track salary payments  

### 📅 Attendance Tracking
- Mark attendance individually or in bulk  
- Generate daily/monthly attendance reports  
- Prevent duplicate attendance marking  

### 💰 Financial Management
- Collect student fees individually or in bulk  
- Pay teacher salaries individually or in bulk  
- Generate financial reports: fees collected, due, salary expenses, net balance  

### 📊 Reporting & Analytics
- Student summary reports  
- Attendance analytics  
- System-wide statistics (students, teachers, average age, experience, etc.)  

### 🗄️ Data Persistence & Logging
- Automatic saving/loading with backup  
- Professional logging system for debugging and auditing  
- Pickle-based data storage (replace with JSON/DB for production)
